Your Daily Queue · Editorial demo

Your feed can be smaller and still be smarter.

Most news products optimize for volume, recency, or engagement. A personalized briefing can optimize for something more useful: what this particular person needs to understand today.

Why this made the queue

This demo puts context before quantity. The goal is not to show everything. It is to show enough, explain why it matters, and make it easy to move on.

Build the information around the human.

Your Daily Queue is a public demo inspired by a real custom morning briefing. The content here is fictional and intentionally generic. The interaction is the point.

Choose the topics. Change the reading depth. Tell the queue what deserves more attention. The interface adapts instead of asking the person to adapt to another endless feed.
